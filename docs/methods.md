# Methods

We explored three complementary approaches:

- Time Series (ARIMA, Kalman):
  - Does the same trend fit the data well for our entire time series? If not, this may suggest policy effects.
  - How well can we predict future housing prices?
- Clustering:
  - Categorize states by their housing price dynamics (percentage changes).
  - Which housing markets are correlated vs. uncorrelated (for diversification)?
- Bayesian Hierarchy:
  - Which states are most favorable for buyers (low intercept, high slope)?
  - How do demographics relate to market responsiveness to shocks?
