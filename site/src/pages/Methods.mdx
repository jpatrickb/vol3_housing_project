Inference via PyMC3 NUTS: 4 chains, 2k tune + 2k draws per chain (8k total). Posterior intervals estimate growth level and rate per state."

<section className="concepts">
  <div className="container">
    <h2>Key Methodological Concepts</h2>
    <div className="concept-grid">
      <ConceptCard
        title="ARIMA Models"
        definition="AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average models capture temporal dependencies in stationary series. Integration (differencing) handles non-stationarity; AR and MA components capture autocorrelation."
      />
      <ConceptCard
        title="VARMAX"
        definition="Vector AutoRegressive Moving Average with eXogenous variables. Extends ARIMA to multiple correlated time series, leveraging interdependencies between state markets and demographic covariates."
      />
      <ConceptCard
        title="Hierarchical Priors"
        definition="Multi-level Bayesian specification where state-level parameters (intercepts, slopes) are drawn from a global distribution, sharing information across states while respecting regional differences."
      />
      <ConceptCard
        title="Time-Series Clustering"
        description="K-means applied to price change patterns groups states with similar dynamics. Enables separate VARMAX models per cluster, improving forecasting by accounting for regional market structure."
      />
      <ConceptCard
        title="Classical Decomposition"
        definition="Separates a time series into trend, seasonal, and residual components. Differencing removes trends; seasonal adjustment isolates cyclical patterns (e.g., summer price peaks)."
      />
      <ConceptCard
        title="Stationarity & Differencing"
        definition="Stationarity means no trend or changing variance. Differencing (subtracting consecutive values) converts non-stationary series to stationary, required by most time-series models."
      />
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section className="takeaways">
  <div className="container">
    <h2>Methodological Insights</h2>
    <ul className="takeaway-list">
      <li><strong>Differencing vs. other detrending:</strong> Linear differencing proved most effective for stationarity compared to moving averages or OLS detrending, providing a robust baseline for downstream modeling.</li>
      <li><strong>Cluster convergence at ~7:</strong> K-means solutions converge around 7 clusters; beyond this, diminishing interpretability as singleton clusters proliferate. This optimal point balances regional granularity with statistical stability.</li>
      <li><strong>VARMAX advantages:</strong> Multivariate models on clustered states improved trend tracking versus univariate ARIMA, demonstrating the value of capturing market interdependencies within regions.</li>
      <li><strong>Hierarchical modeling benefits:</strong> Bayesian hierarchical priors enable borrowing strength across states while preserving regional heterogeneity—a critical balance for small-sample state-level analysis.</li>
      <li><strong>Demographic effects are pre-2020:</strong> The relationships between demographics and housing growth reflect pre-pandemic economic structures. Post-2020 data excluded; model assumptions may not generalize to shock periods.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</section>
